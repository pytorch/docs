# torch._foreach_round_

torch._foreach_round_(*self: [tuple](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#tuple)[[Tensor](../tensors.html#torch.Tensor), ...] | [list](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list)[[Tensor](../tensors.html#torch.Tensor)]*) → [tuple](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#tuple)[[Tensor](../tensors.html#torch.Tensor), ...] | [list](https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#list)[[Tensor](../tensors.html#torch.Tensor)]

Applies [`torch.round()`](torch.round.html#torch.round) in-place to each tensor in `self` and
returns `self`. The returned object is the exact list or tuple passed as input.
