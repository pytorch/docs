This is the quantized equivalent of [`ELU`](torch.nn.ELU.html#torch.nn.ELU).

- **scale** - quantization scale of the output tensor

- **alpha** ([*float*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/functions.html#float)) - the alpha constant
